lite-binary-reader — oscilloscope

A packed binary capture — three i16 channels interleaved at stride 6 — is written into an ArrayBuffer each frame, exactly as an ADC or a wire device would deliver it. LiteBinaryReader decodes it and draws the traces. The render loop reads through laneOf() (a raw Int16Array view) — zero DataView calls, zero allocation per sample.

demo/index.html — repo-only, served with npx serve demo. The reader imports no sibling; this page imports only ../Reader.js.
